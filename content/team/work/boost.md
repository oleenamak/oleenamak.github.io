Boost Protocol is an open-source platform that lets crypto teams create and distribute on-chain incentives to grow product adoption. Backed by Greylock, Electric Capital, and TCG.

As the founding marketer, I led go-to-market through the transition from RabbitHole’s consumer rewards app to Boost’s developer platform. I helped turn the protocol into a product that developers and ecosystem partners could use to launch their own campaigns. My work spanned positioning, product launches, developer education, and partner campaigns. Boost reached 400,000+ wallets across 50+ ecosystem partners.
