American Operator acquires small businesses through an ownership model designed to keep them locally owned and operated. $50M Series B, led by 8VC and Human Capital.

I led the growth function across paid acquisition, lifecycle marketing and CRM, PR, social, and content. Key initiatives included (1) launching Operator Network, a community platform for entrepreneurs looking to buy a small business, (2) setting up operations for Locally Owned and Operated, a certification program for small business owners, and (3) managing PR and communications in-house to secure national media coverage.

---

**Launching and growing Operator Network**
Aspiring business buyers needed practical guidance long before they were ready to finance a deal. To find out what would bring them into Operator Network, I tested audience segments and messaging across channels, including paid media, third-party sponsorship and content. Practical education consistently outperformed community-focused messaging, so we invested more in webinars, office hours, and learning resources.

I built lifecycle campaigns and worked with Product to refine onboarding so we could identify qualified operators after signup. The network grew to 6,000+ qualified operators, and became the backbone of the Operator Talent Pool and Advisor Network today. 

**Scaling Locally Owned and Operated**
The certification program relied on manual outreach and fulfillment, and applications sat in Airtable outside the Deal team’s CRM. I tested Facebook ads as a way to reach more independent business owners and brought in a fulfillment partner to mail certification stickers in batches as application volume grew.

I worked with the Deal team to capture details like industry and expected sale timeline in HubSpot, then launched a Facebook group to give certified owners a reason to stay connected. The program grew to 6,000+ certified businesses, with Facebook generating qualified leads at $4–$5 each and acquisition and fulfillment costing about $10 per certified business.

**Securing national media coverage**
When Mainshares became American Operator, we had to explain an unfamiliar acquisition model to owners weighing what would happen to their business after they sold. I worked with the CEO and Brand team on the relaunch messaging and helped develop our first case study to show how the model worked in practice.

I ran PR in-house and secured coverage in Bloomberg, Fox Business, the New York Post, Inc., and Entrepreneur. Across the broader relaunch, inbound leads grew 15× in one quarter and accounted for about 24% of total lead acquisition.
