American Operator acquires and operates small businesses through a local-first ownership model, offering an alternative to traditional private equity. The firm raised a $50 million Series B led by 8VC and Human Capital.

As Growth Marketing Manager, I led paid acquisition, lifecycle marketing and CRM, PR, social, and content. Key initiatives included launching Operator Network, a community for acquisition entrepreneurs; operationalizing Locally Owned and Operated, a certification program for small-business owners; and bringing PR and communications in-house to earn national coverage.
