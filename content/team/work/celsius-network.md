Celsius Network was a crypto lending platform that served more than one million customers globally.

I led lifecycle marketing through a period of rapid growth and the company’s Chapter 11 restructuring. I built the systems we used to segment customers, automate campaigns, and measure performance. My work spanned lifecycle campaigns, deposit and referral programs, and customer communications during the restructuring.
