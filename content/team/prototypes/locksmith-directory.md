A directory that helps people find an honest, locally owned locksmith.

[![Your Locally Owned Locksmith, Austin map](assets/prototypes/locksmith-directory-austin.jpg)](https://yourlocallyownedlocksmith.com/austin)

Locksmith searches are full of bait-and-switch operators, and it's hard to tell who's legitimate in an emergency. I built a Confidence Score from Texas license records and recent customer reviews, with a person checking every listing.

Live in Austin with 39 locksmiths scored. [yourlocallyownedlocksmith.com/austin](https://yourlocallyownedlocksmith.com/austin)
