Growth marketing case study

American Operator

Building the buyer funnel and acquisition engine for the Operator Network.

Context

American Operator acquires small businesses through an ownership model designed to keep them locally owned and operated. $50M Series B, led by 8VC and Human Capital.

I led the growth function through a business model pivot, helping American Operator build a repeatable way to find qualified buyers and sellers and turn that demand into a pipeline of small-business acquisitions. Channels I owned included paid acquisition, lifecycle marketing and CRM, PR, social, and content.

Why we built the Operator Network

As Mainshares scaled, it needed to drive more deals on the supply side, and buyers can search for years before acquiring. We built the Operator Network to reach them early and nurture them to Mainshares once they were ready to raise capital on a deal.

KPI

  1. Marketing-qualified leads

    Qualified buyers: U.S.-based applicants with intent to buy and enough operating experience.

The buyer funnel

I led the launch and built the acquisition engine, driving rapid experimentation to test channels and messaging. I also owned the customer lifecycle and CRM, building the buyer journey from acquisition to the handoff to Sales.

  1. Acquisition

    Paid media drove most leads. We tested offers from a downloadable guide to a course, community, live events and a newsletter. Practical education consistently beat community messaging.

  2. Onboarding and activation

    Qualified applicants created an account, entered the community and got a welcome drip that led them to the intro video, an introduction post and a first webinar or workshop.

  3. Nurture

    Courses, expert Q&A, weekly webinars and office hours, and a deal-sourcing newsletter kept buyers engaged while they searched.

  4. Sales handoff

    Readiness signals, like a deal under LOI or a request for buyer tools, triggered Sales outreach to bring the deal to Mainshares to raise capital.

Outcome

Within a quarter, the network grew to 6,000+ members, ran 25+ expert-led events, and drove two deals into the opportunity pipeline. The Operator Network members are now the backbone of the Advisor Network and Operator Talent Pool at American Operator.
